OWNDAYS Ticket Management & Asset Management

An internal IT and asset management platform with dedicated Ticketing System and Asset Management workflows, helping teams track requests, manage assets, and review reports clearly and efficiently.

Ticketing System

  • Create tickets for branch and department issues or requests
  • Track statuses such as Pending, In Progress, Completed, and Rejected
  • Assign work and monitor progress
  • Approve or reject requests through the approval workflow
  • Review reports by ticket category and status
  • Export data as CSV for reporting and historical records

Asset Management

  • Store computer, phone, printer, and other IT asset records
  • Track usage status, owners, and branch locations
  • Organize equipment by type and operational needs
  • Track maintenance, transfers, and equipment warranties
  • Support efficient purchasing plans and asset audits
  • Connect with the Ticketing System to resolve asset-related issues
Start the application with npm run dev and open http://localhost:5173
